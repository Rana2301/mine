# 🎂 Happy Birthday & 1-Year Anniversary Romantic Website 💖

A romantic, customizable React website built to celebrate your girlfriend's birthday and your **1-Year Relationship Milestone**.

---

## ✨ Features Included

1. **💍 Our 1-Year Journey & Storyline Timeline**:
   - **Chapter 1**: First glance at **College Seminar Hall 3** 🏛️
   - **Chapter 2**: The Instagram DM — asking for college notes (*"Me aeni pasethi notes mangela!"*) 📱📚
   - **Chapter 3**: Late night chats & falling in love 🌙
   - **Chapter 4**: Celebrating **1 Complete Year (365 Days) of Togetherness** 🥂

2. **🎂 Interactive Birthday Cake Cutting & Candle Blowing**:
   - Realistic 3-tier cake with flickering candles.
   - Click to **Blow Out Candles** (produces realistic blow sound & smoke).
   - Click to **Cut Cake** with birthday fanfare jingle & multi-color confetti fireworks!
   - Unlocks secret romantic wish card.

3. **📸 Polaroid Memory Wall & Lightbox Gallery**:
   - Scrapbook polaroids with washi tape & tilt effects.
   - Tag filters (*Seminar Hall 3, College Memories, Cute Dates, 1 Year Special, Birthday Special*).
   - Attached romantic Google-reference quotes to each photo.
   - Interactive heart likes & full-screen lightbox zoom.

4. **💌 Google-Referenced Romantic Quotes Generator**:
   - 25+ curated poetic & heartfelt birthday quotes.
   - Categorized by mood: *Birthday Love, Our Story, Anniversary & Journey, Forever & Soulmate, Romantic Quotes*.
   - **"Surprise Me 🎲"** random quote generator with 3D flip card animation.
   - One-click copy quote button.

5. **✉️ Sealed Love Letters & 10 Reasons Flip Cards**:
   - Interactive *"Open When..."* envelopes.
   - Master Birthday & 1-Year Love Letter with handwritten styling.
   - Interactive flip cards for 10 cute reasons why you love her.

6. **🎁 Love Stats & Birthday Scratch Voucher**:
   - 100% Soulmate Compatibility meter.
   - Interactive scratch card revealing a secret VIP birthday voucher pass.

7. **🛡️ Full-Featured Admin Portal (Passcode Protected)**:
   - Default PIN: `1234` (can be changed in settings).
   - **Photo Manager**: Upload photos directly from your computer/mobile (auto-converts to local base64 storage) or paste Web image URLs, attach quotes, edit & delete photos.
   - **Story Manager**: Customize the Seminar Hall 3 meeting, the notes story, or add new chapters.
   - **Quote Manager**: Add, edit, or delete quotes.
   - **Settings**: Change Girlfriend's name, your name, anniversary date, birthday date, background music, and passcode.
   - **Backup & Restore**: One-click JSON export & import to easily backup or transfer site content.

## 🚀 How to Run the Website

In the project folder (`E:\prtaham\mine`), open your terminal and run:

```bash
npm run dev
```

Then open the local URL in your browser (usually `http://localhost:5173`).
